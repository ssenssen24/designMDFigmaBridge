## Overview

Architectural Minimalism meets Journalistic Gravitas. The UI evokes a
premium matte finish — a high-end broadsheet or contemporary gallery.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in high-contrast neutrals and a single accent color.

- **Primary (#0B71B9):** Deep saturated blue for headlines and core text — strong hierarchy without reading as black.
- **Secondary (#7432FF):** Electric violet for borders, captions, and metadata — pairs with the blues without stealing focus from actions.
- **Tertiary (#0096FF):** Bright sky / cyan blue — high-visibility accent and the sole driver for interaction (buttons, links, focus).
- **Neutral (#EBECEE):** Cool light gray surface — softer than pure white, matte gallery-wall backdrop.

## Typography

Noto Sans KR carries body and headline weight; Space Grotesk handles uppercase
labels and metadata where character distinction matters.
